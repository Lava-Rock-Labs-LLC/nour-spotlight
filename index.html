02Research

Her master's thesis, now in progress at the University of Regina.

Current thesis · In progress

Enhancing Operational Efficiency to Match Supply and Demand at Regina Food Bank

Her thesis applies linear and integer optimization to help the food bank match incoming food with the needs of the families it serves, delivered through a dashboard staff can act on. As a research assistant, she also used logistic regression to understand patterns behind missed appointments.
